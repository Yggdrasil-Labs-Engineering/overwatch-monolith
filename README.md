# Why Monolith Exists

Operational intelligence is only valuable if it can be preserved, trusted, and recalled when it matters most.

Every observation, interpretation, decision, and recommendation generated by the OVERWATCH ecosystem represents knowledge that should never be lost.

Monolith exists to preserve that knowledge.

As the historical archive of OVERWATCH, Monolith stores operational intelligence with integrity, allowing future decisions to benefit from past experience.

Monolith does not observe.

It does not interpret.

It does not judge.

It remembers.

---

# Mission

Monolith preserves trusted operational intelligence for future understanding, decision-making, and continuous improvement.

Every archived record should answer three questions:

- What happened?
- When did it happen?
- Can it be trusted?

# Integrity Principles

Monolith preserves information according to five principles.

## Immutable by Design

Archived intelligence should never be silently modified.

---

## Trusted and Verifiable

Every archived record should be validated for integrity and authenticity.

---

## Accessible When Needed

Historical intelligence must remain searchable, organized, and available.

---

## Context Preservation

Historical records should retain the operational context in which they were created.

---

## Built for Longevity

Operational knowledge should outlive systems, platforms, and individual deployments.
